What's inside

Designed for the way construction actually works.

Every screen is filtered and prioritised for the role looking at it. PMs see one thing, foremen see another, subcons see only what's theirs.

One Project, One View

The dashboard surfaces every critical item at a glance — open tasks, unresolved issues, pending RFIs, active subcontractors. Stop digging through tabs.

Drawing Version Control

Colour-coded status — Approved, In Review, Superseded. Never reference an old drawing again. Every revision is tracked, every change is logged.

Priority-Tagged Issues

Snags raised on site land directly in the PM's hands with HIGH / MED / LOW priority. Assign, comment, and resolve — all in one thread, fully audited.

Direct Line to Site

Chat with foremen, subcons, and consultants in context. Every conversation is attached to the project, never lost in a personal WhatsApp.

Mobile-First, Built for Site

Photos, drawing markups, issue raising — all work the same on a phone in the rain as on a laptop in the office. Offline-tolerant, glove-friendly.

Full Audit Trail

Every task, issue, drawing revision, and approval is timestamped and attributed. When the regulator or your client asks, you have the answer.
